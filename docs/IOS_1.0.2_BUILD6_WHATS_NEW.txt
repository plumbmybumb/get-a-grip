Train in pounds or kilograms. Choose your weight unit in Settings and use it throughout the app, from live readings and targets to maxes and history.

Workouts are easier to follow from across the room: blue edges while you pull, orange when it's time to let go, and a quiet gray edge during rest. The rest label is clearer, and you'll see which hand is up next.

New workout peaks stay with the hand that recorded them. Choose which ones to save as maxes, with left, right and both hands kept separate.
