# Android WH-C06 responsiveness and recovery — 8 September 2026

Android version **1.0.1 (6)** addresses lost WH-C06 scans and declining response
in longer sessions. It is based on build 4's public source. Separate, uncommitted
routine hand-max improvements are excluded. Build 5 was uploaded as a draft during
verification, then replaced before submission when hardware testing exposed the
long-scan downgrade.

## Behavior

The WH-C06 sends readings in advertisements. Previously, a silent scan could stay
registered in app state forever: watchdog calls did nothing, and the silence
timeout changed the screen to Searching without starting another scan.

The hotfix filters advertisements by manufacturer ID while retaining the existing
payload validation and address lock. Sustained silence triggers actual scan
replacement, with three acquisition attempts, a scan-start rate limit and cooldown
for Android error 6. Callbacks from retired scans and observations from before the
new scan cannot contaminate its connection. Searches can be cancelled from the
gauge screen, and optional diagnostics include scan events and relative times.

Hardware testing also confirmed Android's long-scan timeout: the tested phone's
Bluetooth configuration was 300,000 ms, and its diagnostic record marked the older
scan as Forced. The mode summary still reported the original low-latency request.
Android's timeout path downgrades filtered scans to low-power delivery. The app now
renews WH-C06 scan registration every four minutes, preserving its logical
connection, software tare, scale lock and measurement timeline. Ordinary gaps and
stream watchdog calls do not renew scans. A failed renewal follows the same real
disconnect and bounded-recovery path. OEM timeout policies can vary.

The Progressor and other GATT clients, measurement engine, storage, permissions,
background policy, renderer and iOS source are unchanged. No samples are invented
and no timing or signal-freshness thresholds are relaxed. No Bluetooth addresses,
serial numbers or advertisement payloads are added to reports.

## Verification

- All **939 Android tests** pass: 538 app tests and 401 engine tests, no failures
  or skipped tests. This includes 33 direct broadcast-client/filter regressions
  and the search Cancel/Connect UI tests.
- All **600 iOS tests** pass; the shared Swift oracle passes **39 scenarios and
  4,130 steps**. All test simulators were shut down afterward.
- Regressions cover five consecutive renewals (20 virtual minutes), preserved
  tare/address/timing, no invented measurements, errors during renewal, stale
  callbacks, radio loss, cancellation and error-6 cooldown.
- Independent review found no release blockers.
- The optimized side-by-side test app preserved the phone's Play-installed app
  and training data. Real WH-C06 checks verified load tracking, unloaded tare,
  scale-off/on recovery without restarting the app and measurement after an
  app switch. In build 6, Android recorded two consecutive four-minute scans
  with 1,465 and 1,495 results (about 6.1 and 6.2 readings per second), neither
  marked Forced. The user confirmed force response remained prompt beyond the
  previous five-minute slowdown point. Verification used a Realme Android 16
  phone; the reporter's Samsung Android 15 phone has not been tested locally.
- The signed APK has the expected upload certificate and passes 16 KiB packaging
  alignment. App identity, manifest permissions and device requirements match
  build 4. ReTrace mapping is included in the AAB. Scoped secret scans passed.

## Distribution

Submitted to the existing closed Alpha track at 100% rollout. Play Console shows
**Changes in review**, with automated quick checks running before review. Managed
publishing is off, so eligible testers receive build 6 after approval. Build 4
remains live until then. The release preview showed no blocking errors and no
devices losing compatibility. The existing dependency native-symbols warning
remains; ReTrace mapping is attached.
