The session screen is new. The force trace now fills the whole screen, and the numbers sit on one Liquid Glass panel that takes the colour of the moment: blue while the clock runs, amber when it is waiting on you. During a rest the countdown fills the screen, big enough to read from the wall.
• iPad: in landscape the graph takes the whole display, with your grip, the next pull and the controls in one column beside it.
• Apple Watch: the whole face is the colour of the state — blue to pull, green while the clock runs, red to re-grip, orange before a different grip.
• In Low Power Mode the digits snap instead of sliding, to spare the battery. The clocks keep counting.
• A gauge that delivers its readings in bursts no longer empties the graph.
• The live gauge is one tap from Today, and it now looks like the session screen: the trace fills the display, the numbers sit on one glass panel, the actions on one dock.
• History adds an all-time card: sessions, pulls, time under tension, volume, days trained, your heaviest pull.
• A session that runs past midnight counts for the evening it belonged to. Your training day now turns at 4 am.
• Deleting a routine also deletes its sessions, and Undo brings both back.
• Settings opens on the gauge picker, so choosing among the gauges the app works with is the first thing you see.
