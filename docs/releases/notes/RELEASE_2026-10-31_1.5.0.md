# Release record — iOS 1.5.0 (30) and Android 1.5.0 (19)

Status (31 Oct 2026, evening): **prepared on `main` at `c0d128b`, not archived, not uploaded,
not pushed to origin.** What's New: [docs/releases/1.5.0/](../1.5.0/) (iOS, English and
French) and [ANDROID_1.5.0_WHATS_NEW.txt](ANDROID_1.5.0_WHATS_NEW.txt).

## What is in it

- **History session detail** — a row opens a read-only summary of that workout (stats, per-pull
  rows, facts, maxes saved), with Export and Delete + Undo; climbs and hand-logged hangs show
  only what was logged. Both platforms.
- **"Hardest 5 % of time" trend** — a second chart per grip beside the average
  (`TrainingLoad.topLoadKg`, `Fixtures/trend/top-load.json`, both engines). The seed now has
  warm-up pulls so the two charts differ in demo data.
- **Target load as % of critical force** — `TargetBasis` on the plan and sets,
  `CriticalForceTable` with the `MaxTable` rules, builder chips, CSV v5 columns
  (`target_basis`, `cf_at_start_kg`, `cf_reference`), `Fixtures/target-basis/`,
  `docs/TARGET_BASIS.md`. **No CloudKit schema deploy, no Room migration** (the routine-level
  column was dropped; the basis rides in the sets blob). **Older builds read a CF band as % of
  max — ship both platforms together.**
- **Gauge** — the live gauge and max test re-kick a silent stream themselves (Wake only after
  three failed re-kicks), Reset clears peak/average/timer/pulls. **Demo mode removed** from
  release builds (mock only behind DEBUG `-mockDevice`); the Play listing's "explore demo
  mode" clause removed; App Review notes rewritten around "Start without a gauge".
- **Settings › Sound** (GitHub issue 7): cue tones on/off and level, haptics untouched.
- **Holds 0.6 s** (GitHub issue 9), both holds kept. Issues 6 and 8 declined.
- **Body-weight field always has a Done** (user report, Thomas); **Benchmarks large-text layout**
  (user report); **Dynamic Type / font-scale sweep** on both platforms (~35 findings).
- **iPhone Duo** — rails clear of the vertical tab bar; two-column Today/Benchmarks and
  two-pane History when open (also changes iPad); grip picker as a safe-area card where no
  top cutout exists; the live runner may rotate on iPhone and draws the iPad column layout at
  phone scale (`docs/IPHONE_DUO_NIGHTSTAND.md`); Duo store screenshots in
  `appstore/out_duo/` (outer repo, uncommitted).
