## What is in it

- **The side column on iPhone Duo** (`SideColumns.swift`, `docs/IPHONE_DUO.md`). Beside the
  system's vertical tab bar, cards on all four tabs end 12 pt short of the glass, Today's and
  History's decks run under it, and charts reach the card's edge with their kg labels moved
  left. A tap on Today's neighbour card brings it forward and never starts a session.
- `SideColumns` reads `reservedRegions` behind `#if compiler(>=6.4)`, so main still compiles
  on CI's Xcode 26.6, which has no 27.1 SDK.
- No `Sources/Model` or `Shared` change, so **no CloudKit schema deploy is owed**.
