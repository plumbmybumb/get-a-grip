Fixed the 20-pull limit: sets now support up to 100 pulls per hand.

Maxes is easier to use:
• Measure again opens the gauge straight away.
• Measure your left and right hands in one visit, or save just one hand.
• Edit values by hand, review earlier records, and see how a new max changes your routine targets.
• Use the + button to add a max for another grip.

A few smaller additions:
• Preview a routine’s grips, timing and hand order before you start.
• Tap Last 14 days to open your history.
• During longer rests, see your next hand and grip with a larger countdown. The graph stays visible.
• Tidier Settings, with links to the Grip Connect and Crimpdeq open-source projects.
